YouLikeIt is a desktop content creation tool that helps a single creator turn cinema, manga, and music analysis into reviewed shorts before uploading drafts to their own TikTok and YouTube accounts.

See the dashboard How it works
● Local-first
● No cloud database
● OAuth scoped per creator
● Drafts only — never auto-publish

What it does

A focused workflow built around one creator's craft. No agencies, no bulk operations, no automated public posting.

📚

Source-aware narration

Pulls thematic context from TMDb, AniList, Last.fm and Fandom wikis so each script is rooted in real material — not hallucinated.

🎙

Voice studio

Three distinct narrator personas with their own tone, pacing and subtitle styles. Generated locally with edge-tts.

🎞

Visual matcher

Gemini Vision picks the most fitting panel or still per narration segment, sourced from licensed Fandom imagery and your local library.

✅

Human review queue

Every short lands in a review dashboard. Approve, disapprove, or send back for edits. Nothing leaves your machine without a click.

🚀

Draft uploads

Approved videos are pushed as unlisted on YouTube and drafts on TikTok — final edits and music selection happen inside each native app.

🔁

Rotation engine

A rotation table cycles through topics and personas every three days so the same theme isn't beaten to death.

From idea to posted draft

Each step is auditable. The creator owns every decision before any platform sees the work.

  1. 01

    Pick a topic

    Rotation engine suggests a series, film or artist that hasn't been covered recently.

  2. 02

    Generate scripts

    An LLM writes three short scripts grounded in cataloged themes and iconic moments.

  3. 03

    Render locally

    Audio, subtitles and visuals are composited into a 9:16 video on the creator's own machine.

  4. 04

    Review

    Each video opens in the review dashboard with rating, notes, frame-level issues and recommended sounds.

  5. 05

    Upload as draft

    Approved videos go to YouTube unlisted or to the TikTok creator inbox for final edits before public posting.

What we ask for, and why

Each scope has a single, narrow purpose. Tokens are stored locally on the creator's device and never sent to a YouLikeIt server (we don't run one).

▶

YouTube

  • youtube.upload — publish the creator's own approved drafts as unlisted shorts.
  • youtube.readonly — show the creator their own channel inside the dashboard.
  • yt-analytics.readonly — surface retention curves so the creator learns what works.
♪

TikTok

  • user.info.basic — show the connected creator's display name in the dashboard.
  • video.upload — push the approved video to the creator's TikTok inbox as a draft for them to finish in the mobile app.
