2. Description of the Service

YouLikeIt is a desktop content-creation tool that helps a single creator produce, review, and publish short-form video content. It integrates with third-party platforms (YouTube, TikTok) only to upload the creator's own approved content as drafts or unlisted videos. There is no public website that hosts user-uploaded content and there is no multi-tenant cloud database.

3. Authorization & Scopes

To upload content the creator authorizes the Service via OAuth on each platform. Authorization is limited and revocable.

The Service never publishes content publicly on the creator's behalf without an explicit click. The creator may revoke access at any time from the platform's connected-apps settings.
