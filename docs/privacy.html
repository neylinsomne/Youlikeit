YouLikeIt - Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2, 2026

1. Overview

YouLikeIt ("we", "our", "the Service") is a personal content creation and publishing tool. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that data.

2. Data We Collect

2.1 Data from TikTok

When you authorize YouLikeIt to connect to your TikTok account, we access:

DataPurposeStored?
Open IDIdentify your account for uploadsLocally only
Display nameShow in our dashboardLocally only
AvatarShow in our dashboardNot stored
OAuth tokensAuthenticate API requestsLocally, encrypted

2.2 Data from YouTube

When you authorize YouLikeIt to connect to your YouTube account, we access:

DataPurposeStored?
Channel infoUpload videos to correct channelLocally only
Upload permissionUpload videos on your behalfToken stored locally
Analytics (read-only)Track video performanceLocally only

2.3 Content You Create

Videos, narrations, and metadata you produce using the Service are stored locally on your machine. We do not upload your content to our servers.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your data exclusively to:

  • Upload video content to your authorized accounts as drafts or unlisted videos
  • Display your account information in the local review dashboard
  • Track content performance through platform analytics APIs

4. Data Storage and Security

All data is stored locally on your device. YouLikeIt does not operate cloud servers or databases. OAuth tokens are stored in local JSON files within the project directory. We recommend:

  • Not sharing your tokens/ directory
  • Adding tokens/ to your .gitignore
  • Revoking access from platform settings if you stop using the Service

5. Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, or transfer your data to any third party. The only data transmission occurs directly between your device and the platforms you have authorized (TikTok, YouTube).

6. Data Retention

Data persists on your local device until you delete it. You can remove all stored data by:

  • Deleting the tokens/ directory (removes all OAuth tokens)
  • Deleting the data/ directory (removes analytics and content data)
  • Revoking app access from TikTok Settings > Security > Manage app permissions
  • Revoking app access from Google Account > Security > Third-party apps

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: View all data stored locally in the project directory
  • Delete: Remove any or all stored data at any time
  • Revoke: Disconnect your platform accounts at any time
  • Portability: All data is in standard formats (JSON, SQLite) and fully portable

8. Children's Privacy

The Service is not intended for use by anyone under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. Changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.
