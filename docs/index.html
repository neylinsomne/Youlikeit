YouLikeIt

Personal content creation and publishing platform for short-form video.

What is YouLikeIt?

YouLikeIt is a personal content management tool that helps creators produce, review, and publish short-form video content across multiple platforms including YouTube Shorts and TikTok. It uses AI-assisted narration, automated editing, and smart scheduling to streamline the content creation workflow.

How it works

Creators use YouLikeIt to generate narrated video content from manga, anime, and cinema sources. Videos are reviewed in a dashboard before being uploaded to creator accounts as drafts or unlisted content for final editing and publishing from the native platform apps.
