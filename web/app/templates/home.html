How it works

1 · Explore

Find films by what they make you feel

Each emotion has a short guide: what it is, how cinema shows it, and a mindful pause to notice it in yourself.

2 · Understand

See the same feeling across many stories

We connect key scenes from different films that express the same emotion or situation — a farewell, a betrayal, a moment of hope — so patterns become visible.

3 · Share

Creators turn scenes into short lessons

Creators prepare narrated vertical lessons from public-domain or properly licensed footage and send them to TikTok as drafts, where they review and decide what to publish.

Why film?

Stories let us rehearse emotions at a safe distance. Watching how characters face loss, fear or betrayal gives us words for our own experience — and helps us recognize the same feelings in the people around us. That is empathy: understanding from the inside.

Our library is built from public-domain films and footage that creators are licensed to use. See our Terms of Service for content rules.
